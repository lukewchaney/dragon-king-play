Paint

Click or drag to paint. Rectangle and Outline: drag from corner to corner (Outline is good for walls). Fill: paints every touching square of the same kind. Right-click clears. Ctrl+Z undoes. Only one Start.

Building pictures

Each building letter uses one front picture. A block of the same letter is one building; its picture goes on the side facing the most road or plaza (the gold edge on the grid).
